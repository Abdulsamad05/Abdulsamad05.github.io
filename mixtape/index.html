Finance platform in daily use

A platform a self-employed client uses every day for income, invoices, expenses and UK tax.

The brief
My client was running her finances from spreadsheets. She needed one place for income, invoicing, expenses and tax that she could trust.
My part
Combined three data sources into one structure: a direct bank feed, photographed receipts read with OCR, and forwarded invoice emails. Shipped four AI features in small releases: receipt OCR with categorisation, invoice filling from forwarded emails, overdue-invoice chasing and savings plans.
The result
Tracked use after release. Two AI features are used constantly and one is barely touched, and that changed what I built next. Caught a tax figure that was wrong while every automated test passed, by checking the output against published HMRC guidance, then corrected the logic. Found and fixed six security weaknesses, including a login with no attempt limit, nine routes reachable without signing in, and a webhook that would accept a forged message. Traced a live incident where an email loop sent over 400 messages and fixed it the same day.

About

Liner notes

I'm an engineer who works across AI, data and security. For the past year I've built and run a finance platform that a self-employed client uses every day. That work taught me to ship in small releases, track what people actually use, and check outputs against an independent source rather than trusting the tests.

Before that I finished an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Stirling, after a BSc in Cybersecurity at Robert Gordon University. I like problems where the answer has to be right, not just plausible.
